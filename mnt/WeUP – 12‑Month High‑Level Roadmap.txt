WeUP – 12‑Month High‑Level Roadmap
*Goal: Take the UI‑first prototype through the full Phase‑0 ladder, deliver Phase‑0.15, 0.20, 0.25 and 0.30+, then launch Phase 1 as version 1.24.
*Version cadence – two public releases each month (early‑month “E” and late‑month “L”) – incrementing the minor version by .01 on each ship.
*Start date: July 2026 (Month 1).
________________


Versioning Scheme
Release
	Meaning
	v1.0 – v1.23
	Incremental feature, stability and observability drops.
	v1.24
	Final Phase‑1 “city‑scale, revenue & intelligence” release.
	All releases are fully CI‑tested, semver‑compatible (no breaking API changes after v1.0) and ship to a staging environment for stakeholder review before production rollout.

	What It Delivers
	Why It Matters
	Multi‑City Expansion
	City‑partitioning service, district taxonomy, market‑freeze rules, auto‑provisioning of venue‑ & sponsor‑data
	Allows WeUP to launch in any new city without code changes
	Advanced Personalisation
	Real‑time signal‑fusion (P50) + collaborative‑filtering ranking (P51)
	Drives higher engagement and sponsor ROI
	GeoAudio
	Ambient‑sound ingestion pipeline, venue‑audio matching heuristics (P52)
	Differentiator for night‑life discovery
	Sponsor Marketplace
	Campaign builder, billing & invoicing (P46), KPI dashboards (P48)
	Direct revenue stream for the platform
	Partner API
	Public, versioned OpenAPI spec, rate‑limiting, API‑key management
	Opens ecosystem for 3rd‑party curators
	Compliance & Privacy
	GDPR data‑export, consent UI, audit‑log retention (P15)
	Legal risk mitigation for scaling
	Observability & Reliability
	OpenTelemetry collector, structured logs, alerting, automated canary releases
	Guarantees SLA as traffic grows
	All Phase 1 capabilities are feature‑flag‑gated at launch, giving the product team the ability to roll them out incrementally post‑v1.24.
