1. Vision Recap – The Core User Story
Step
	Desired Feeling
	How WeUP Makes It Real
	Discover
	“I see only the events I’m meant to see – no spam.”
	3‑tier invite system, trust‑graph gating, privacy‑preserving discovery, multi‑modal (audio, video, AR) signals.
	Unlock
	“I reveal an event when I’m ready – my identity stays private.”
	Vouch‑decay, event‑scoped anonymity, zero‑knowledge attendance proofs, deferred reveal “ghost‑pin”.
	Buy
	“Purchase is instant, secure and fair.”
	Stripe‑first checkout, dynamic pricing, ticket‑spine QR, on‑chain hash anchor, incentive‑program rewards.

	“The venue knows the crowd, but I’m not tracked.”

	“I can engage without being surveilled.”
	End‑to‑end privacy layer, differential‑privacy analytics, counts‑not‑traces metrics, ambient‑light signalling.
	Persist
	“Only the artifacts that matter survive.”
	Event‑scoped shop, ritual‑triggered NFTs, memory‑artifact tokens, post‑event portals, lineage‑linked residual payouts.

8. Closing Summary
WeUP will evolve from a rock‑solid ticket spine into a privacy‑first cultural operating system that treats events as finite, tokenised experiences. By layering trust‑graph discovery, spatial mapping, native commerce, wearables, selective blockchain, and finally a protocol‑level AI‑augmented infrastructure, we create a unique, efficient, and emotionally resonant user journey.
The tabled roadmap, the 30+ innovative concepts, and the rigorous documentation & release‑gate process together guarantee:
1. Scalable efficiency – door validation, offline pathways, and real‑time analytics keep operations lean.
2. Privacy & safety – ZK proofs, differential privacy, no surveillance heatmaps.
