WeUP Sponsors Portal — FRD
Houston-first, fractal-local, anti-extraction sponsorship system


1. Product Vision & Constitutional Constraints
The Sponsor Portal allows brands to fund infrastructure, creator grants, supplies, and tools for verified cultural spaces. Sponsors participate by delivering value, not mass advertising.


Anti-NASCAR Rule: The system strictly forbids forced logo placements on creator assets and mandatory "wrap" requirements (e.g., "post X times"). Sponsor presence is exclusively functional.


Anti-Surveillance Rule: Individual-level ad targeting is completely blocked. All sponsor interactions, claims, and attendance validations are mediated by privacy-preserving zero-knowledge proofs and Merkle trees.


2. User Personas
Sponsor Operator: Deploys capital into funding pools, reviews cryptographically verified "bubbled" opportunities, and monitors aggregated escrow milestones.


Venue / Organizer: Curates sponsor coalitions, tracks the delivery of physical or digital infrastructure, and processes verifiable receipts to unlock payouts.


Creator / Attendee: Interacts with the sponsor via opt-in micro-loops (claiming seeded tools, redeeming credits, or fulfilling volunteer payouts) without sacrificing behavioral privacy.


3. Core Functional Flows
3.1 Opportunity "Bubbling" (Discovery)
System Action: Instead of an open feed, the system surfaces a "bubbled" slate of opportunities (projects, events, venues, creator-circles).


Logic: Opportunities only become visible to sponsors once they hit a deterministic local receipt density threshold, validating that the cultural space is real and ready.


Sponsor Action: Sponsors browse these slates to identify optimal sections for funding without seeing individual user identities.


3.2 Program and Offer Management
Sponsor Action: A sponsor creates a Program that dictates the parameters of a funding pool (e.g., a multi-event liquor program at a specific venue).


Execution: The sponsor attaches specific Offers to the program, such as bar credits or creator product seeding.


System Action: Upon program approval, the required capital is locked in a chain-agnostic smart contract escrow (Stellar or Solana) to guarantee accountable funding.


3.3 The Opt-In Claim & Redemption Loop
User Action: Verified attendees or creators selectively opt-in to claim an offer.


System Action: The system issues a claim token. By default, all claim tokens are non-transferable.


Redemption: When the user redeems the token at the venue, the WeUP Signal Integrity Protocol (WSIP) processes the redemption receipt securely against the event graph.


3.4 Milestone Escrow Release
System Action: Payouts are highly deterministic and strictly receipt-based, eliminating manual dispute resolution.


Trigger: Once a required volume of redemption or delivery receipts is cryptographically verified, the system automatically unseals the escrow and routes the payout to the venue or organizer.


4. Abuse Controls & Ecosystem Integrity
To maintain the integrity of the funding pipeline, the system enforces the following automated constraints:


Rate Limits: Hard limits on claim rates per persona, per time window.


Anomaly Detection: The system monitors redemption velocity and receipt patterns to flag non-personal fraud flags per section.


Coalition Caps: Enforces strict section density limits to prevent any single sponsor from monopolizing a venue and turning it into an ad grid.


5. Phased Release Sequence
Phase 1 (Core)


Deploy the bubbling slate for Opportunities and Programs.


Enable basic Offer/Claim loops and receipt integration.


Support manual coalition planning and a single escrow path (Stellar or Solana).


Phase 2 (Coalitions + CRA)


Activate full Coalition objects and the CRA optimizer.


Enforce section caps and weights, generating internal-only Fair Attribution (φ) reports.


Phase 3 (Venue Liquor Programs)


Unlock venue-direct, multi-event sponsorship layers with built-in compliance gating.


Map physical delivery receipts directly to automated milestone-based releases.


Phase 4 (Festival Pop-Up Cells)


Support zone-based sections and higher-frequency re-optimization algorithms for large footprint deployments.


Implement offline synchronization considerations for remote festival environments.
